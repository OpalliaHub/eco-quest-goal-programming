The drill starts from a fixed mid-match state. It follows the printed attack
costs and damage and the one-manual-attachment rule. It leaves draws, the Bench,
Prize cards and Weakness outside its scope. The deck inventory coefficients and
practice points are classroom assumptions. The project is a classroom fan activity.
