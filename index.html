        MATH GUIDE

        What makes a good plan?

        Build your plan

        Check limits, then score goals

        For each goal, calculate shortfall = max(0, target − output). A smaller weighted score is better.

        The search keeps every feasible plan with the lowest score. If a goal has weight zero, check both shortfalls separately even when the score is zero.
